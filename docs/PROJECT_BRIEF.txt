You are the lead full-stack engineer, AI engineer, Web3 engineer, UX engineer, accessibility engineer, database architect, and DevOps engineer for this project.

I am participating in a 36-hour hackathon and I will actively assist you while building.

PROJECT NAME:
SANAD

TAGLINE:
"Understand your situation. Prove what matters. Find the support you need."

CORE CONCEPT:
SANAD is a multilingual AI-powered government and support-services navigator combined with a portable verifiable credential system.

The user should be able to explain their situation naturally, even using mixed languages, phonetic spelling, Arabizi, Hinglish, Hindi-English, Urdu-English, or Arabic-English.

SANAD understands the situation, converts it into structured facts, asks for clarification where necessary, lets the user confirm the interpretation, creates a verifiable record/credential of the confirmed claim, identifies potentially relevant government/support services from a curated verified database, guides the user through the service journey, allows the credential to be attached as evidence, tracks the application, announces meaningful interface/status changes accessibly, and allows an external party to independently verify the credential.

IMPORTANT:
This must NOT look like a generic chatbot.
This must NOT look like a crypto dashboard.
This must NOT be two unrelated products glued together.

The entire product should feel like ONE coherent platform.

3. IMPORTANT TRUST MODEL
==================================================

Do NOT incorrectly claim:

"The blockchain proves that the user's claim is true."

That is NOT what the system proves.

Instead distinguish:

USER_REPORTED
AI_EXTRACTED
USER_CONFIRMED
ISSUER_VERIFIED
CREDENTIAL_VALID
CREDENTIAL_REVOKED

The credential proves that a particular record/claim was issued and has not been altered or revoked.

The underlying factual truth can only be established through evidence or an authorized issuer.

Every relevant UI should communicate this distinction.

Use language such as:

"User-confirmed claim"

"Credential issued"

"Credential valid"

"Potentially eligible"

"Based on the information provided"

Never make definitive legal eligibility claims unless the official source explicitly supports them.
